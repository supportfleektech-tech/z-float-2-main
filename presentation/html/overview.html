THE PROBLEM

Kenyan businesses run money on scattered tools

Money in, money out — separately

•  Payouts on one portal, M-Pesa paybill statements in another

•  Manual matching of who paid which invoice

•  No single balance the team trusts

Tax compliance went digital

•  Every sale needs an eTIMS invoice or receipt

•  Expenses without an eTIMS invoice are no longer deductible (Finance Act 2025)

•  Penalty: double the tax due or KES 2M

A phone number is not an identity

•  Numbers are shared, recycled, and mistyped

•  Two “John Otieno”s in the payee book

•  Buyers need their KRA PIN on invoices to claim VAT

Sources: Tax Procedures Act as amended by Finance Act 2025 (s.23A, eTIMS); KRA income & expense validation notice.

KRA ETIMS · NEW

Electronic tax invoices & receipts, signed by KRA

What the business gets

•  Tax invoices, receipts and credit notes with VAT A–E computed per line

•  Automatic eTIMS receipt for every payment received

•  Buyer KRA PIN printed so customers can claim input VAT

•  Shareable link + QR that verifies on the KRA portal

How it works

•  OSCU API: selectInitOsdcInfo → cmcKey (encrypted at rest) → saveTrnsSalesOsdc

•  Sequential invoice numbers per device under a row lock

•  KRA outage? Documents queue and retry; rejections surface for a human

•  Signed documents are immutable in the database — corrections are credit notes

CONTROLS & TRUST

Built like a bank ledger, not a spreadsheet

Double-entry ledger

•  Every credit and debit is a balanced journal; a health check proves it every 10 minutes.

Maker-checker

•  Payments, reversals, new payees and privileged roles need an independent approver.

Idempotency everywhere

•  API keys, provider callbacks (CheckoutRequestID, TransID) and bank refs dedupe.

Transactional outbox

•  Receipts, notifications and webhooks are published exactly once, after commit.

Immutable fiscal records

•  A database trigger blocks edits/deletes of KRA-signed documents.

Secrets & audit

•  Device keys AES-GCM encrypted; callbacks token-checked; every change audited.

GO-LIVE & ROADMAP

From sandbox to production

Go-live checklist

•  KRA: apply for OSCU (or VSCU) on the eTIMS portal / GavaConnect; receive device serial

•  Set ETIMS_DRIVER=oscu, ETIMS_ENVIRONMENT=production

•  Map items to KRA item classification codes (placeholder today)

•  Safaricom Daraja production app: STK passkey, C2B URL registration

•  Set MPESA_C2B_CALLBACK_TOKEN; enable external validation

•  Run the certification test cases in the KRA sandbox first

Roadmap
