Tax Procedures Act, Finance Act 2025

From the 2025 accounting period KRA validates declared income and expenses against eTIMS data on iTax. Failing to issue an eTIMS invoice costs double the tax due or KES 2 million, whichever is higher.

Every business that collects on M-Pesa now needs a compliant invoice or receipt for each sale — and a way to link it to the payment.
